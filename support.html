FAQFrequently Asked Questions

Is RuntimeX a substitute for dive computer decompression?

No. RuntimeX is a planning tool to be used before your dive, not during it. Always dive with a certified dive computer as your primary decompression reference. Computed plans may differ from your computer's real-time calculations.

What's the difference between Bühlmann and VPM-B?

Bühlmann ZHL-16C is a dissolved-gas model: it tracks inert gas absorbed by tissue compartments and calculates allowable ascent based on tolerable supersaturation, tuned with gradient factors. VPM-B is a bubble model: it accounts for the nucleation of microbubbles during the ascent and tends to prescribe shallower, longer first stops. Neither is universally "better" — choose the model that matches your training.

Where are my saved plans stored?

Plans are saved as JSON files in the app's private documents directory on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. If you uninstall the app, plans are deleted along with the app data unless you export them first.

How do I back up my plans?

Export any plan as a PDF using the share button, then save it to your cloud storage of choice (iCloud, Google Drive, etc.). A direct JSON export/import feature is tracked in the GitHub issues.

Does RuntimeX support imperial units (feet / psi)?

Currently the app uses metric units (metres, bar, litres). Imperial unit support is a requested feature — add a thumbs-up to the relevant GitHub issue to help prioritise it.

The PDF export does not appear in the share sheet — what do I do?

Make sure the app has storage/files permission on your device. On iOS, you may need to tap "Save to Files" in the share sheet if no direct app targets appear. If the issue persists, open a bug report on GitHub with your OS version and device model.
